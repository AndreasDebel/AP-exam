# Glossary

**Section**
A named part of the venue with a fixed capacity. The unit a buyer chooses, and the unit that is assigned to one database.

**Place**
One unit of a section's capacity. Places are interchangeable and have no seat number.

**Buy**
A buyer taking 1 to 4 places in one section in a single step. Refused if fewer places are free. There are no holds: nothing is reserved before the buy.

**Sold**
A place that has been bought. It never returns to the section.
