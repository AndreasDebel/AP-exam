# Glossary

**Section**
A named part of the venue with a fixed capacity. The unit a buyer chooses, and the unit that is assigned to one database.

**Place**
One unit of a section's capacity. Places are interchangeable and have no seat number.

**Hold**
A buyer's temporary claim on up to 4 places in one section, valid for about 10 minutes. A buyer has at most one hold.

**Confirm**
The buyer turning 0 to 4 places of their hold into sold places. Any places not confirmed return to the section.

**Sold**
A place that has been confirmed. It never returns to the section.
